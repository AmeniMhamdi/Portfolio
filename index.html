      about

      Quality is not a phase at the end. It's a decision made every sprint.

      I design and maintain automated test suites with Playwright, Selenium and Cucumber, in TypeScript and Java. The work rarely starts with code: it starts with understanding the business flow, deciding what is actually worth automating, then writing BDD scenarios the product team can read without a translator.

      I have worked on insurance platforms, Smart Metering systems and Android interfaces — always in Agile Scrum, always sitting close to the developers. Pipelines, defect triage, static analysis, message queues: the visible output is a test report, but the real output is everything that never reached production.

      Based in Ariana, Tunisia. I work in Arabic, French and English.
